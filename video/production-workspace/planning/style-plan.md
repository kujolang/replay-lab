- **Canvas** 1920 × 1080 at 30 fps. Use a 12-column grid with 120 px side margins and at least 108 px vertical margin. Keep essential copy inside title safe.
- **Palette** Charcoal `#101010`, near-black terminal `#171717`, near-white ink `#f5f5f5`, secondary `#bdbdbd`. Use one inverted near-white panel for the held-out proof. Fine rules at 1–2 px separate meaningful groups. Underline or outline the focus so the sequence remains legible in grayscale.
- **Typography** Use a local sans with heavy weights and a sober local monospace. Use 76–110 px headings, 118–180 px proof numbers, 32–40 px primary terminal and chart data, and 28 px minimum for captions and secondary labels intended to be read. Wrap the 64-character digest on an even boundary if needed.
- **Editorial rhythm** Cut at 12, 25, 38, 55 and 70 seconds. Use a character-safe line wipe for initial terminal fields, a receipt-like reveal for the cassette, successive diagram steps for strict replay, and an ordinal trace for the candidate sweep. Settle before any read window. The rejected candidate gets a decisive mark. The held-out bars land, then cut back to the matching request. Hold the ending repository and test command for at least four seconds.
- **Voice and audio** Narration is the clock. Use a calm, clear local speech voice and time each reveal to its anchor word after measuring the original segments. Leave short gaps at cut points. Use a low instrumental bed only if locally available and rights are traceable. Reserve restrained action cues for cassette match, sweep lock and gate rejection. Keep dialogue dominant.
